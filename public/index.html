About Reading Tree

An independent project

Reading Tree is a personal, independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints or Intellectual Reserve, Inc.

The text

Scripture verses are shown for personal, non-commercial reading only. Chapter headings, summaries, introductions, footnotes and other study helps are not included.

Fruits, tokens and colors

Fruits, tokens and highlighter colors are rewards inside this app. They have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money.

Your information

We keep your username, a scrambled (hashed) version of your password, your country and city, your reading progress, fruits, tokens and highlighter colors, and the notes and highlights you write. We do not sell your information, show ads, or share it with anyone. The site owner can see this information in the project's database. Never share your password.

Questions or deleting your data

To correct or delete your information, please contact the site owner.
